# Other languages

The 100 rules describe patterns of thought and structure, so they apply in every language. Only the word lists differ. For any language without its own file:

1. Read `rules.en.md` and treat each listed phrase as a meaning, not a string.
2. Build the target-language equivalents of the banned openers, closers, transitions, stock adjectives and press-release phrases. Use the ones native speakers recognize as machine-flavored, not literal translations.
3. Do not write calques from English. Typical carriers: "not only … but also" templates, "plays a crucial role", "in today's fast-paced world", "delve into", "a testament to", "seamlessly", "empower", "unlock", "journey". Rule 91 applies in both directions.
4. Keep the register of the genre in that language: a Spanish error message, a French legal notice and a Japanese chat reply have their own conventions. Follow those, not an English template.

## Starting points per language

These are examples of what to look for, not complete lists.

| Language | Typical machine-flavored phrases |
|---|---|
| Español | "No solo X, sino también Y", "juega un papel crucial", "en el panorama actual", "sumérgete en", "un testimonio de", "Espero que esto te ayude", "¡Claro!" |
| Français | "Non seulement X, mais aussi Y", "joue un rôle central", "dans le paysage actuel", "plongeons dans", "J'espère que cela vous aide", "Absolument !" |
| Italiano | "Non solo X, ma anche Y", "svolge un ruolo fondamentale", "nel panorama attuale", "immergiamoci", "Spero che questo ti aiuti" |
| Português | "Não apenas X, mas também Y", "desempenha um papel crucial", "no cenário atual", "mergulhe em", "Espero ter ajudado" |
| Nederlands | "Niet alleen X, maar ook Y", "speelt een cruciale rol", "in het huidige landschap", "Ik hoop dat dit helpt" |
| Polski | "Nie tylko X, ale także Y", "odgrywa kluczową rolę", "w dzisiejszym krajobrazie", "Mam nadzieję, że to pomoże" |
| Türkçe | "Sadece X değil, aynı zamanda Y", "kilit bir rol oynar", "günümüzün dinamik dünyasında", "Umarım yardımcı olur" |
| 日本語 | 「Xだけでなく、Yも」、「重要な役割を果たします」、「いかがでしたでしょうか」、過剰な敬語の連鎖 |
| 中文 | “不仅X，而且Y”、“起着至关重要的作用”、“总而言之”、“希望对你有帮助” |

Rules about structure (contrast templates, groups of three, recaps, fake balance, explained punchlines, stage directions, inflated significance) carry over without change.
